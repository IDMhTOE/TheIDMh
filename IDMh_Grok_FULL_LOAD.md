## 2. Kernel (do not rewrite lower counts)
ONE is Rotatte[Base] = {{1/sqrt(2)}, {0}}.
A radian is the orthogonal rotation. A half-radian is not orthogonal, so it is not an observable reference by itself. The author names that half-radian Rotatte, little radian. It is undividable.

Zero-sum symmetry (ZSS): cannot get something from nothing; get two somethings that sum to nothing. Pair production. Complete observable set sums to one Rotatte[Base]. Unique means orthogonal. Not orthogonal means the same object.

Two Rotatte make one radian and the base frequency of a de Broglie complex quantum harmonic oscillator (dBCQHO / dBQHO), standing wavelength 1. Observation starts at count 2, when a second orthogonal direction exists. Before that the brick is in superposition with nothing.

Iteration is powers of two because pairs double: complex, quaternion, octonion, sedenion. Sedenions and above have zero divisors; the author treats that boundary as the slide from discrete arrows to a continuum.

Immutability: higher constructs stretch or carry lower ones. They do not edit them. A count-7 residual cannot be rotated away at count 11.

Author creed fragment: probability of finding one is one; a probability needs at least two. That is why observation is a two-valued wave.
